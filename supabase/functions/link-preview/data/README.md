Place centroids and Japanese aliases derived from GeoNames cities15000 (downloaded 2026-10-09), licensed CC BY 4.0: https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/readme.txt and https://www.geonames.org/ . Includes all Japanese cities in that dataset and world cities with at least 100,000 residents or national-capital status. The added Kanto prefecture names use their government-seat centroids; Tokyo districts/stations use approximate named-place centres. These coordinates indicate a place mentioned in a post, not a verified device location.

mapStations.json contains actual railway-station coordinates and explicit station-name aliases from GeoNames JP.zip, downloaded 2026-10-10. Rebuild with `python3 scripts/build-map-stations.py /path/to/JP.zip`. Bare city aliases are excluded. Source: https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/JP.zip (same CC BY 4.0 license). General Tokyo/prefecture mentions are not placed at artificial city-centre coordinates.
